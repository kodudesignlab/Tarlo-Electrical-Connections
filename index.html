About Harry

Same bloke, every job

I'm Harry Betts. I run Tarlo Electrical Connections on my own out of Brookvale, and I've been in the trade for 10 years. When you book a job, I'm the one who turns up, does the work and signs off on it. No unsupervised apprentices, no subcontractors you've never met.

I answer my own phone. If I'm up a ladder I'll call you back the same day. You get a fixed price before I start, and the site gets left the way I found it.
